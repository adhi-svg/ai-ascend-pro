# FieldFix Business Model

## Platform Overview
FieldFix connects customers with local technicians for home services through a smart booking system.

---

## How It Works

### For Customers:
1. Customer searches for a service (plumbing, electrical, etc.)
2. Two booking options:
   - **Auto-Assign**: System automatically assigns highest-rated available technician
   - **Manual Select**: Customer browses profiles and chooses technician manually
3. Customer pays **booking confirmation fee** upfront
4. Technician receives booking request and accepts/rejects
5. Once accepted, technician comes to location
6. Work is completed
7. Customer pays technician for **work/labor charges directly**

### For Technicians:
1. Technician registers and gets verified
2. Profile is visible to thousands of local customers 24/7
3. Receives booking notifications via:
   - **Auto-assignment** based on rating & availability
   - **Manual selection** by customers browsing profiles
4. Can accept or reject bookings based on availability
5. Customer has already paid confirmation fee before technician arrives
6. Technician completes work and charges customer for labor/materials directly
7. Earnings deposited to in-app wallet

---

## Revenue Model

### Platform Fee Structure:

#### Booking Confirmation Fee (Platform Revenue):
- Customer pays booking confirmation fee when booking
- **20% goes to FieldFix platform**
- **80% goes to technician wallet**
- Technician can withdraw wallet balance anytime

#### Work/Labor Charges (100% to Technician):
- Customer pays technician directly for:
  - Labor charges
  - Material costs
  - Any additional work done on-site
- **FieldFix takes 0% of work charges**
- This is direct payment between customer and technician

### Example Breakdown:

**Scenario**: Customer books plumber for pipe repair

1. **Booking Confirmation Fee**: ₹200
   - Platform: ₹40 (20%)
   - Technician Wallet: ₹160 (80%)

2. **On-Site Labor Charge**: ₹1,500
   - Platform: ₹0 (0%)
   - Technician: ₹1,500 (100%)

3. **Material Cost**: ₹800
   - Platform: ₹0 (0%)
   - Technician: ₹800 (100%)

**Total Technician Earns**: ₹160 (wallet) + ₹1,500 + ₹800 = **₹2,460 from this job**

## Key Features

### Two Booking Systems:

1. **Auto-Assignment** (Rating-Based):
   - Algorithm assigns jobs to highest-rated available technicians
   - Factors: Rating score, acceptance rate, proximity, availability
   - Technicians with 4.5+ ratings get priority
   - Faster bookings for top performers

2. **Manual Selection** (Customer Choice):
   - Customer views technician profiles
   - Can see: ratings, reviews, experience, specializations
   - Customer selects preferred technician
   - Good for repeat customers or specific requests

### Payment Flow:

1. **Upfront Confirmation**:
   - Customer pays booking confirmation fee when booking
   - This ensures serious bookings only
   - Reduces no-shows and cancellations

2. **Platform Split**:
   - 20% to platform (covers app, support, marketing, insurance)
   - 80% to technician wallet immediately after confirmation

3. **Direct Work Payment**:
   - Technician quotes labor charges on-site
   - Customer pays directly (cash, UPI, card)
   - Platform never touches this money

4. **Wallet Withdrawal**:
   - Technicians can withdraw wallet balance anytime
   - Transferred to bank account within 24 hours
   - No minimum withdrawal limit
